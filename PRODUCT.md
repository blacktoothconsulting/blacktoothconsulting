## Product

**Wyoming Clinic of Integrated Health** — a single-location, multi-disciplinary health clinic website. The site is a marketing-and-conversion surface, not a patient portal: its job is to explain the three service lines, build trust, and turn a visitor into a booked appointment.

## Audience

Prospective and returning local patients, in three overlapping groups:

- **Chiropractic patients** — pain relief, adjustments, ongoing musculoskeletal care.
- **Medical care patients** — primary/integrated medical visits with the clinic's medical provider (Gordon).
- **Massage clients** — therapeutic and relaxation massage.

Most visitors arrive on mobile, are deciding whether to call/book, and want three things fast: what you treat, when you're open, and how to book.

## Purpose (jobs to be done)

1. Route a visitor to the right service (chiropractic / medical / massage) quickly.
2. Get them to book — online for medical (embedded scheduler), by phone or appointment-request form for chiropractic and massage.
3. Establish credibility (providers, transparent medical pricing, Google reviews).
4. Provide practical logistics — hours, address/map, phone.

## Operating context

- **Framework:** Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind v4, shadcn/ui.
- **Primary surface:** mobile portrait. Every UI decision is judged at ~375px first.
- **Booking model is intentionally mixed:**
  - Medical → embedded third-party online scheduler (with a mobile-friendly "open in new tab" fallback).
  - Chiropractic & massage → phone + server-backed appointment-request form.
- **Email delivery:** contact/appointment-request submissions send through Resend (`RESEND_API_KEY`, `RESEND_EMAIL_DOMAIN`).
- **Reviews:** links out to the clinic's Google listing — read reviews (Google search results) and leave a review (`g.page/r/CS3mz2OaQCStEBM/review`).
- **Deploy flow:** work happens on a preview branch; Production is only updated on explicit publish.

## Hours (source of truth)

- **Chiropractic:** Mon, Wed, Thu 8am–6pm · Tue 8am–5pm · Fri 8am–12pm.
- **Medical:** Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri 8am–6pm · Tue 8am–5pm.

These values are duplicated across the hero info card, contact section, mobile menu, and SEO structured data — keep them in sync when they change.

## Constraints

- No fabricated social proof. Testimonials link to real Google reviews rather than showing placeholder/empty-star cards.
- Booking CTAs must respect the service the visitor is on (no generic "Book" that dumps a chiropractic visitor into the medical scheduler).
- Preserve existing branding, palette, and typography — refinements, not redesigns.
- Accessibility: semantic landmarks, sr-only labels, 44px touch targets, 16px+ inputs (no iOS zoom).

## Voice

Warm, plainspoken, and local. Reassuring and practical, not clinical-corporate or salesy. Copy should reduce friction ("here's how to book") rather than hype.
